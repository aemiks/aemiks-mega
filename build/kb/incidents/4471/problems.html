How it is counted

  • Report ids are unique: the export pages overlap, so repeated rows count once.
  • A report is placed by its tenant, not by the intake tag (a guess from the reporter’s browser).
  • hint: a form booking at a tenant whose booking form prints a month-first hint.
  • one-day: reports the desk read as a one-day move (desk-notes.json). Their dates move by one day in the same month, which no day/month order explains, so they are not counted under parser.
  • Every number on this page is computed from the data at render time. When the desk merges more reports into #4471, the table follows.

What changes for the desk

  • Do not close #4471 when the parser fix ships: check the hint and one-day tickets first.
  • A new report about a wrong date goes to the problem its tenant and channel point to, not to the ticket with the same subject.
